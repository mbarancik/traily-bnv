# About BNV Trails

BNV Trails are a community-built mountain bike trail network in Bardejovská Nová Ves.
They are developed by enthusiasts who dedicate long-term effort to trail cleaning,
surface maintenance, building safe features, and improving the riding experience throughout the season.

## What you will find here

We currently offer **9 marked trails**:

- **6 downhill trails**
- **3 uphill climbs**

The network is diverse — from smooth flow sections for beginners to technical parts
that challenge experienced riders. In this area, you can put together short recreational laps
or a longer training day with multiple runs.

# Ride responsibly

![Warning](Warning.svg)

- Enter at your own risk.
- A helmet is mandatory; gloves and knee, elbow, and back protectors are strongly recommended.
- Trails are one-way. Riding outside them or modifying them in any way is prohibited.
- Always check current trail conditions before riding and adapt your speed to the terrain.
- After rain, some sections may be soft or slippery.

## Cleanliness and respect

Please keep the area clean — what you bring in, take out.
Ride carefully, stay alert, and do not overestimate your skills,
because hikers, mushroom pickers, or children may be present not only near the trails,
but also directly on them.
