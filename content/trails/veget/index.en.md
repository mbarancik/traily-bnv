Veget is a wide and clear machine-built flow trail (with a more natural character in the lower section) with a compact dirt surface, berms, and small rollers, making it ideal for beginners.
The trail is suitable for all mountain bike types, including hardtails and e-bikes.
It is downhill-only — to climb back up, use Prepojka and either Výšľap Hore Hore or Výšľap pre labužníkov.
