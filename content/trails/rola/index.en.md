Ro-Ľa is a moderately difficult trail that alternates between natural and shaped sections.
The first part has berms through a pine corridor and then transitions into terraces and shallow gullies.
In the second half, expect tighter switchbacks, narrow cuts, and a technical finish with a small gap and a sharp turn.
